# AgenticCity

**An open research initiative for agent-based urban systems.**

Building toward interconnected urban simulations,
starting with mobility.

AgenticCity explores how agents with different roles, information,
and memories interact through shared urban environments,
and how their decisions shape collective outcomes.

## Starting with mobility

Our first implementation is AgenticMobility, a research platform
connecting LLM travelers, traffic managers, and a shared
MATSim environment.

Current work focuses on route and departure-time choices,
personal memory, congestion pricing, and day-to-day feedback.

Explore AgenticMobility:
https://github.com/Jiaheng-Xiao/AgenticMobility

## Research directions

- Individual adaptation and collective outcomes.
- Information, memory, and policy feedback.
- Reproducible evaluation of agent-based systems.
- Connections between mobility and other urban systems.
